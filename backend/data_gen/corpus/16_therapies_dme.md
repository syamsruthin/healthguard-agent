# Rehabilitation Therapies, Durable Medical Equipment, Home Health & Skilled Nursing

## Physical and occupational therapy
- Covered for injury, illness or after surgery when the therapy is expected to improve function.
- **Visit limit:** **30 combined physical and occupational therapy visits per plan year** (rehabilitation), plus a separate 30 visits for habilitation (keeping or learning skills, e.g., for developmental delays).
- **Prior authorization is required for visits beyond 20** in a plan year; your therapist submits progress notes showing measurable improvement.
- Cost sharing: your plan's specialist copay per visit (deductible then coinsurance on HDHP and Catastrophic plans).
- Visits beyond the limit are denied with **D007 — benefit maximum reached**; your provider may request a limit exception with clinical documentation.
- **Cardiac rehabilitation** after a heart attack, bypass surgery or stent counts toward the therapy limit only after the first 36 sessions are exhausted; the first 36 sessions are always covered.

## Speech therapy
Covered up to **20 visits per year** for speech, language and swallowing disorders.

## Chiropractic care
Spinal manipulation is covered up to **20 visits per year** on Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum plans (specialist copay). Not covered on Catastrophic plans.

## Durable medical equipment (DME)
- Covered when prescribed by a provider for use in the home: CPAP machines, wheelchairs, walkers, hospital beds, insulin pumps, nebulizers, blood glucose monitors.
- **Prior authorization is required for any item over $500** and for all rentals beyond 3 months.
- Cost sharing: deductible then coinsurance.
- CPAP coverage continues after the first 90 days only if usage data shows compliance (at least 4 hours per night on 70% of nights).

## Home health care
Intermittent skilled nursing and therapy visits in the home are covered up to **60 visits per year** with **prior authorization** when you are homebound. Custodial care is not covered.

## Skilled nursing facility (SNF)
Covered up to **60 days per benefit period** after a qualifying hospital stay, with prior authorization and concurrent review. Custodial and long-term care are excluded.

## Hospice
Hospice care for members with a terminal illness and a life expectancy of six months or less is covered **at no cost**, including respite care.
