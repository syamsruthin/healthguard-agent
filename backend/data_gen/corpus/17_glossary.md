# Glossary of Health Insurance Terms

**Accumulators** — Running totals of how much you have paid toward your deductible and out-of-pocket maximum this plan year.

**Allowed amount** — The maximum amount HealthGuard recognizes for a covered service. In-network providers accept it as payment in full.

**Balance billing** — When an out-of-network provider bills you for the difference between their charge and the allowed amount. Prohibited in situations covered by the No Surprises Act.

**Benefit period / plan year** — The 12-month period your benefits and accumulators apply to. HealthGuard 2026 plans run January 1 – December 31, 2026.

**Coinsurance** — Your share of the cost of a covered service, calculated as a percentage of the allowed amount (for example 20%), usually after you meet the deductible.

**Copay (copayment)** — A fixed dollar amount you pay for a covered service, such as $35 for a primary care visit. On HealthGuard copay plans, copays apply without meeting the deductible first.

**Deductible** — The amount you pay for covered services each plan year before HealthGuard starts paying (except for services with copays and preventive care).

**Embedded deductible** — On family plans, each person has an individual deductible inside the family deductible; one person never pays more than the individual amount.

**EOB (Explanation of Benefits)** — A statement explaining how a claim was processed. It is not a bill.

**EPO (Exclusive Provider Organization)** — A plan that covers in-network care only (except emergencies) and does not require referrals.

**Formulary** — The list of prescription drugs covered by your plan, organized into tiers.

**HDHP (High-Deductible Health Plan)** — A plan with a higher deductible that qualifies you for a Health Savings Account.

**HMO (Health Maintenance Organization)** — A plan that covers in-network care only (except emergencies) and requires a PCP and referrals to specialists.

**HSA (Health Savings Account)** — A tax-advantaged savings account for medical expenses, available with an HSA-qualified HDHP.

**In-network** — Providers and facilities contracted with HealthGuard.

**Medically necessary** — Services needed to diagnose or treat an illness, injury, condition or its symptoms that meet accepted standards of medicine.

**Out-of-pocket maximum (OOP max)** — The most you pay for covered in-network services in a plan year (deductible + copays + coinsurance, including prescriptions). After reaching it, HealthGuard pays 100% of covered services for the rest of the year. Premiums do not count.

**PCP (Primary Care Physician)** — A family medicine, internal medicine or pediatrics provider (or NP/PA) who provides and coordinates your routine care.

**PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)** — A plan that covers both in-network and out-of-network care (at a higher cost) without referrals.

**Premium** — The monthly amount you pay for your health plan.

**Prior authorization** — Approval from HealthGuard required before certain services or drugs are covered.

**Referral** — An order from your PCP to see a specialist; required on Core HMO plans.

**SBC (Summary of Benefits and Coverage)** — A standardized document summarizing a plan's costs and coverage.

**Specialist** — A physician or provider focusing on a specific area of medicine (e.g., cardiology, dermatology).

**Step therapy** — A requirement to try a lower-cost drug before a more expensive one is covered.

**Tier 1 / Tier 2 providers** — Network designations based on quality and cost-efficiency; Tier 1 providers are preferred.
