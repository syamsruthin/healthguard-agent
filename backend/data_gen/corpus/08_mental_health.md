# Mental Health & Substance Use Disorder Benefits

HealthGuard covers mental health and substance use disorder (MH/SUD) care **at parity with medical and surgical care**, as required by the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act. Your cost sharing, visit limits and authorization requirements for MH/SUD services are never more restrictive than for comparable medical services.

## Outpatient care

- Therapy and counseling with psychologists (PhD/PsyD), licensed clinical social workers (LCSW), licensed professional counselors and psychiatric nurse practitioners
- Psychiatric evaluation and medication management
- Intensive outpatient and partial hospitalization programs
- **Cost sharing:** the same as a specialist office visit (your plan's specialist copay). On Saver HDHP and Catastrophic plans, deductible then coinsurance applies.
- **No visit limits** and **no prior authorization** for routine outpatient therapy.
- **No referral needed** — even on Core HMO plans, you can self-refer to an in-network behavioral health provider.

## Telehealth therapy

Video therapy sessions are covered the same as in-person sessions. More than 80% of in-network psychiatrists, psychologists and social workers offer telehealth. Use the provider directory filter `telehealth_available` to find one.

## Inpatient and residential care

Inpatient psychiatric care and residential substance use treatment require **prior authorization** (except emergency admissions, which require notification within 24 hours). Cost sharing is the same as an inpatient medical stay.

## Substance use disorder treatment

Covered services include detoxification, medication for opioid use disorder (buprenorphine/naloxone, methadone in certified programs, naltrexone), outpatient counseling and residential treatment. Naloxone rescue spray is covered at $0.

## Crisis support

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — call or text 988, 24/7.
- HealthGuard Behavioral Health Line — 1-800-HG-MENTAL (1-800-446-3682), 24/7, staffed by licensed clinicians who can arrange urgent appointments.
- Mobile crisis services and crisis stabilization units are covered as emergency services.

## Autism services

Applied behavior analysis (ABA) therapy for autism spectrum disorder is covered with prior authorization, with no annual dollar limit.

## Finding care quickly

If you cannot get an appointment with an in-network behavioral health provider within **10 business days**, call Member Services. HealthGuard will arrange a network exception so you can see an out-of-network provider at in-network cost sharing.
