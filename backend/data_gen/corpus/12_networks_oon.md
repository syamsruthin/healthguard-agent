# Networks, Out-of-Network Coverage & Balance Billing

## In-network vs. out-of-network
**In-network** providers have contracts with HealthGuard and accept the allowed amount as payment in full. **Out-of-network** providers have no contract; they may charge more than the allowed amount and bill you for the difference (*balance billing*), except where the No Surprises Act applies.

## Coverage by plan type
- **Choice PPO and Saver HDHP:** out-of-network care is covered after a **separate out-of-network deductible** (twice the in-network deductible) at a **higher coinsurance** (in-network coinsurance + 20 percentage points, maximum 60%). Balance billing may apply.
- **Core HMO:** only in-network care is covered (except emergencies). You must choose a **primary care physician (PCP)**, and specialist visits require an active **referral** from your PCP. Specialist claims without a referral are denied with **D010**. Behavioral health, OB/GYN, preventive care and emergency care do not need a referral.
- **Select EPO:** only in-network care is covered (except emergencies), but **no referrals** are needed. Out-of-network claims are denied with **D009**.

## Network tiers
Within each network, providers are designated **Tier 1** (preferred — high quality and cost-efficiency scores) or **Tier 2** (standard). On 2026 plans, Tier 1 and Tier 2 providers have the same cost sharing; Tier 1 designation is shown to help you choose high-value providers.

## Finding in-network providers
The provider directory lists each provider's `networks` (Choice PPO, Core HMO, Select EPO), specialty, languages, whether they are accepting new patients, telehealth availability, ratings and the typical wait for a new appointment (`next_available_days`). Facilities (hospitals, urgent care, imaging centers, labs, surgery centers) are listed separately. **Always check that the provider participates in the network named on your ID card.** Saver HDHP plans use the Choice PPO network.

## Network adequacy and exceptions
If no in-network provider of the needed specialty is available within reasonable travel time (30 miles / 30 minutes in urban areas, 60 miles / 60 minutes in rural areas) or within appointment-wait standards, call Member Services to request a **network exception**. Approved exceptions are paid at in-network cost sharing.

## Continuity of care
If your provider leaves the network while you are in an active course of treatment (for example, pregnancy, cancer treatment, a scheduled surgery or a serious and complex condition), you may continue seeing that provider at in-network cost sharing for up to **90 days**, or through postpartum care if you are pregnant.

## Balance billing protections
Balance billing is prohibited for emergency care, air ambulance and out-of-network clinicians at in-network facilities (No Surprises Act). See *Emergency Care, Urgent Care & the No Surprises Act*.
