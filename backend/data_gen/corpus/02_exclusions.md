# Policy Exclusions & Limitations

The following services, treatments and supplies are **not covered** under any HealthGuard plan unless explicitly stated otherwise in the member's Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC) or Evidence of Coverage (EOC).

## 1. General exclusions

### 1.1 Cosmetic and elective procedures
Cosmetic surgery and procedures performed primarily to improve appearance rather than restore bodily function are excluded. Examples include rhinoplasty for aesthetic purposes, blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery) without documented visual-field impairment, liposuction, abdominoplasty and hair transplantation. **Reconstructive surgery** required because of accidental injury, mastectomy (including symmetry procedures under the Women's Health and Cancer Rights Act) or a congenital anomaly **is covered** when medically necessary and prior authorized.

### 1.2 Experimental and investigational treatment
Treatments, drugs or devices classified as experimental, investigational or unproven by the FDA or HealthGuard's Clinical Review Board are excluded, including Phase I and II clinical-trial drugs and gene therapies not approved for general use. Routine patient costs (office visits, labs, hospital care) for members enrolled in an **approved clinical trial** for cancer or another life-threatening condition **are covered** as required by the Affordable Care Act.

### 1.3 Non-emergency out-of-network care on HMO and EPO plans
Core HMO and Select EPO plans do not cover non-emergency services from out-of-network providers. Such claims are denied with code D009. Exceptions: emergency services, care when no in-network provider is reasonably available (an approved **network exception**), and services covered by the No Surprises Act (see *Emergency & Urgent Care*).

### 1.4 Custodial and long-term care
Custodial care — assistance with activities of daily living such as bathing, dressing, eating and toileting — is not covered. Skilled nursing facility care is covered for up to **60 days per benefit period** with prior authorization; assisted-living and residential care that is not skilled care is excluded.

### 1.5 Private-duty nursing
Private-duty nursing in the home or hospital is excluded. Intermittent skilled home health visits are covered (60 visits per year, prior authorization required).

## 2. Weight-loss surgery and medications

**Bariatric surgery** (gastric bypass, sleeve gastrectomy) is covered **only on Gold and Platinum plans**, with prior authorization, when the member has a BMI of 40 or higher (or 35 or higher with a serious comorbidity such as type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea) **and** has completed a 6-month medically supervised weight-management program. It is not covered on Catastrophic, Bronze, Silver or Saver HDHP plans.

Weight-management **medications** prescribed for obesity alone (for example Wegovy or Zepbound) are tier 4 drugs that require prior authorization and step therapy. GLP-1 drugs prescribed for type 2 diabetes (Ozempic, Mounjaro, Trulicity) follow the diabetes criteria in the *Pharmacy Benefits* guide. Nutritional counseling is covered at no cost as preventive care.

## 3. Reproductive health

Assisted reproductive technology — in-vitro fertilization (IVF), GIFT and ZIFT — and related fertility medications are **excluded**. **Diagnostic testing** to evaluate infertility **is covered** under the specialist benefit. Contraception, sterilization and family-planning counseling are covered at no cost as preventive care.

## 4. Prescription drug exclusions

- Drugs not on the HealthGuard formulary, unless a **formulary exception** is approved.
- Over-the-counter drugs, vitamins and supplements, except ACA preventive items prescribed by a network provider (e.g., aspirin for cardiovascular prevention, folic acid, prenatal vitamins) which are covered at $0.
- Drugs used for cosmetic purposes (e.g., hair growth, wrinkle treatment).
- Quantities above the formulary quantity limit unless an exception is approved.

## 5. Behavioral health exclusions

Court-ordered evaluations or treatment that do not meet medical-necessity criteria, wilderness and adventure therapy programs, and educational or vocational services are excluded. All medically necessary mental health and substance use disorder services are covered at parity with medical benefits.

## 6. Dental, vision and hearing

Routine adult dental care and routine adult vision care (eye exams for glasses, frames and lenses) are excluded from the medical plan and are available through the HealthGuard Dental and HealthGuard Vision riders. **Pediatric** dental check-ups (2 per year) and pediatric eye exams with glasses (1 per year) for members under 19 are covered at no cost. Hearing aids are covered on Gold and Platinum plans only (one per ear every 36 months). Dental services needed to treat an accidental injury to sound natural teeth within 72 hours are covered under the emergency benefit.

## 7. Travel and international services

Services outside the United States are not covered except emergency care. Repatriation and international air ambulance services are excluded; members traveling abroad should buy travel medical insurance.

## 8. Other exclusions

Acupuncture (except up to 12 visits per year on Platinum plans), massage therapy, services provided by a family member, charges for missed appointments, and services for which no charge would be made in the absence of insurance are not covered.
