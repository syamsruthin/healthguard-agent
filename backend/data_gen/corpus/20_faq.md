# Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I find out how much of my deductible I've met?**
Your accumulators (deductible and out-of-pocket amounts met) are updated as claims are processed. Ask the assistant "How much of my deductible have I met?" or check the member portal. Claims that are pending are not yet counted.

**Why was my claim denied?**
Every denied claim has a denial code (D001–D012) on the EOB with a suggested next step. The most common are D002 (no prior authorization), D009 (out-of-network on HMO/EPO), D010 (no HMO referral) and D004 (duplicate — no action needed). See *Claims Filing, Processing & EOB*.

**How long do I have to appeal?**
180 days from the denial for an internal appeal; 4 months after the final internal decision for external review.

**Do I need a referral to see a specialist?**
Only on Core HMO plans. Choice PPO, Select EPO and Saver HDHP members can self-refer to in-network specialists.

**Is my doctor in-network?**
Check the provider directory — each provider lists the networks they participate in. Your network is on your ID card (Saver HDHP uses the Choice PPO network).

**What's the difference between my deductible and my out-of-pocket maximum?**
The deductible is what you pay before coinsurance starts; the out-of-pocket maximum caps everything you pay in a year (deductible + copays + coinsurance + prescriptions).

**Are telehealth visits covered?**
Yes — at the telehealth copay (often lower than an office visit); behavioral health telehealth costs the same as in-person.

**Can I use urgent care out of network?**
On Choice PPO and Saver HDHP plans, yes (at out-of-network cost sharing). On Core HMO and Select EPO plans, out-of-network urgent care is not covered unless you're traveling outside the service area. Emergencies are always covered.

**When can I change plans?**
During open enrollment (November 1 – January 15) or within 60 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth, a move or losing other coverage.

**Is my GLP-1 medication covered?**
Ozempic, Mounjaro and Trulicity are covered for type 2 diabetes with prior authorization (tier 3). Wegovy and Zepbound for weight management are tier 4 with prior authorization and step therapy.

**What does it mean if my claim says "pending"?**
The claim has been received but not yet processed. Clean claims are decided within 30 days.

**Who can see my information through the HealthGuard assistant?**
Only you (and, for subscribers, your dependents on the same plan). The assistant cannot access other members' records.
