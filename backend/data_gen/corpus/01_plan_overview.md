# HealthGuard 2026 Plan Overview

HealthGuard Insurance offers individual and family medical plans for plan year 2026 across 21 states. Every plan combines a **network product** (which providers you can see) with a **metal tier** (how you and the plan share costs) and a **variant** (Value, Standard or Plus).

## Metal tiers

Metal tiers describe the share of average costs the plan pays (actuarial value). Higher tiers have higher premiums but lower deductibles and copays.

- **Catastrophic** (~60% AV): available to members under 30 or with a hardship exemption. Very high deductible equal to the out-of-pocket maximum ($10,150 in 2026). Preventive care and the first **3 primary care visits** each year are covered before the deductible; telehealth visits are $0.
- **Bronze** (~60% AV): lowest premiums among standard tiers, high deductible, 40% coinsurance.
- **Silver** (~70% AV): moderate premiums and deductibles, 30% coinsurance.
- **Gold** (~80% AV): lower deductibles, 20% coinsurance, fixed copays for imaging, outpatient surgery and inpatient stays.
- **Platinum** (~90% AV): highest premiums, lowest deductible and 10% coinsurance.

## Variants

- **Value** — about 8% lower premium, roughly 15% higher deductible and 20% higher copays than Standard.
- **Standard** — the reference design for each tier.
- **Plus** — about 9% higher premium, roughly 15% lower deductible and copays.

## How copays and the deductible interact

On copay-based plans (all non-HDHP Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum plans) office visits, telehealth, urgent care, emergency room visits, diagnostic tests and tier 1–3 prescriptions are charged a **flat copay that does not require meeting the deductible first**. Services without a copay (for example advanced imaging on Bronze and Silver plans, home health care, durable medical equipment) are charged **deductible, then coinsurance**.

On **Saver HDHP** plans there are no copays: all services except preventive care are paid at the full allowed amount until the deductible is met, then coinsurance applies. This design makes the plan eligible to pair with a Health Savings Account (HSA).

## Family coverage

Family plans have an embedded individual deductible and out-of-pocket maximum plus a family deductible and family out-of-pocket maximum equal to twice the individual amounts. Once the family amount is met, the deductible (or out-of-pocket maximum) is satisfied for every covered family member for the rest of the plan year.

## Plan identifiers

Plan IDs follow the pattern `TYPE-METAL-VARIANT`, for example `PPO-SLV-PLS` is Choice PPO Silver Plus and `HDHP-BRZ-VAL` is Saver HDHP Bronze Value. Your plan ID appears on your member ID card and in the member portal. The exact cost sharing for every plan is listed in that plan's Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC).
