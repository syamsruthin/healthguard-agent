# Telehealth & Virtual Care

## What is covered
- **Virtual primary and urgent care** visits (video or audio) with in-network providers or the HealthGuard Virtual Care service (24/7, average wait under 15 minutes)
- **Telehealth specialist** visits when clinically appropriate
- **Behavioral health** therapy and medication management by video (see *Mental Health & SUD Benefits*)
- E-visits through the provider's patient portal and remote patient monitoring for chronic conditions

## Cost
- Medical telehealth visits are charged your plan's **telehealth copay** (for example $0 on Platinum and Catastrophic plans, $10–$30 on other tiers). On Saver HDHP plans, telehealth is subject to the deductible.
- Telehealth behavioral health visits are charged the same as in-person mental health visits.
- Lab tests or prescriptions ordered during a virtual visit follow normal cost sharing.

## Prescriptions
Providers can e-prescribe to any in-network pharmacy or to HealthGuard Home Delivery Pharmacy. Controlled substances follow federal and state telehealth prescribing rules.

## When telehealth is not appropriate
Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room for chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, severe bleeding or other emergencies. Telehealth providers will direct you to in-person care when needed.

## Out-of-state use
You can use HealthGuard Virtual Care while traveling within the United States. Telehealth with your regular provider while you are in another state depends on the provider's licensure in that state.
