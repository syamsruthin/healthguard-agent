# Emergency Care, Urgent Care & the No Surprises Act

## Emergency care
An **emergency medical condition** is one with acute symptoms severe enough that a prudent layperson with average knowledge of health and medicine could reasonably expect that the absence of immediate care would seriously jeopardize health, impair bodily functions or cause serious dysfunction (the *prudent layperson standard*). Coverage is based on your symptoms, not the final diagnosis.

- Emergency room care is covered **at any hospital, in or out of network, without prior authorization**, on every plan — including Core HMO and Select EPO.
- You pay your plan's **emergency room copay** (or deductible/coinsurance on HDHP and Catastrophic plans). **The ER copay is waived if you are admitted.**
- **Out-of-network emergency care is paid at in-network cost sharing**, and what you pay counts toward your in-network deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.
- Emergency ambulance transport (ground or air) is covered at in-network cost sharing.
- If admitted to an out-of-network hospital, HealthGuard may arrange transfer to an in-network facility once you are stable (post-stabilization care).

## The No Surprises Act
The federal No Surprises Act protects you from **surprise balance bills**:
1. **Emergency services** from out-of-network providers and facilities.
2. **Air ambulance** services from out-of-network providers.
3. **Non-emergency services by out-of-network providers at in-network facilities** — e.g., an out-of-network anesthesiologist, radiologist or assistant surgeon at an in-network hospital or surgery center.

In these situations you pay **only your in-network cost sharing**, and the out-of-network provider **may not balance bill you** for the difference. The EOB will note that the claim was processed under the No Surprises Act. If you receive a balance bill in a protected situation, do not pay it — call Member Services or the federal No Surprises Help Desk at 1-800-985-3059.

**Good-faith estimates:** if you are uninsured or self-pay, providers must give a good-faith estimate of charges in advance; you can dispute bills that exceed the estimate by $400 or more.

## Urgent care
Use urgent care for conditions that need prompt attention but are not emergencies — sprains, minor cuts, fevers, ear infections, strep throat, urinary tract infections.
- You pay your plan's **urgent care copay** at in-network urgent care centers.
- On **Core HMO and Select EPO** plans, **out-of-network urgent care is not covered** (claims are denied with code D009) unless you are traveling outside the service area. Use the provider directory (`facility_type = 'Urgent Care'`) to find an in-network center.
- HealthGuard Virtual Care is a lower-cost alternative for many urgent issues.

## ER or urgent care?
| Go to the ER for | Go to urgent care for |
|---|---|
| Chest pain or pressure | Sprains and strains |
| Trouble breathing | Minor burns or cuts needing stitches |
| Signs of stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty) | Fever, flu or cold symptoms |
| Severe bleeding, head injury, seizures | Sore throat, ear pain, pink eye |
| Severe allergic reaction | Urinary tract infection symptoms |
