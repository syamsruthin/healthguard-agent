# Preventive Care Covered at No Cost

Under the Affordable Care Act, HealthGuard covers recommended preventive services **at no cost to you — no copay, coinsurance or deductible — when you use an in-network provider**. This applies on every plan, including Catastrophic and Saver HDHP plans. (Core HMO and Select EPO plans cover preventive care in-network only; Choice PPO and Saver HDHP plans pay out-of-network preventive care at the out-of-network coinsurance level.)

## Adults
- One annual **preventive (wellness) visit**
- Blood pressure, cholesterol (lipid panel) and type 2 diabetes screening for at-risk adults
- **Colorectal cancer screening** starting at age 45 (stool-based test, or screening colonoscopy every 10 years). A follow-up colonoscopy after a positive stool test is also preventive.
- **Breast cancer screening** — mammogram every 1–2 years for women 40 and older
- **Cervical cancer screening** — Pap test every 3 years (21–65) or HPV co-testing every 5 years (30–65)
- Lung cancer screening (low-dose CT) for adults 50–80 with a qualifying smoking history
- Depression and anxiety screening, alcohol and tobacco use screening and counseling
- Tobacco cessation programs, including FDA-approved cessation drugs (e.g., varenicline) at $0
- **Immunizations**: influenza (annual), COVID-19, Tdap, shingles (50+), pneumococcal, HPV, hepatitis B
- HIV screening and **PrEP** (emtricitabine/tenofovir) for people at increased risk
- Obesity screening and nutrition / weight-management counseling

## Women
- Well-woman visits, contraception (all FDA-approved methods), sterilization
- **Prenatal care** visits and pregnancy-related screenings (see *Maternity & Newborn Care*)
- Breastfeeding support, counseling and a breast pump per pregnancy
- Osteoporosis screening for women 65+ (or younger at increased risk)

## Children (under 19)
- Well-child visits on the Bright Futures schedule (newborn through age 21)
- All routine childhood immunizations
- Vision and hearing screening; **pediatric eye exam and glasses** (1 per year)
- **Pediatric dental check-ups** (2 per year), fluoride varnish
- Developmental, autism and lead screening

## When a preventive visit is not free
If a preventive visit also addresses a new or existing problem (for example, you ask your doctor to evaluate knee pain during your annual physical), the problem-oriented part of the visit may be billed as a separate office visit with the normal copay or deductible. Diagnostic tests ordered because of symptoms are not preventive.

## HDHP note
On Saver HDHP plans, preventive care is covered before the deductible, as permitted by IRS rules for HSA-qualified plans.
