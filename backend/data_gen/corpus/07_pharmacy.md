# Pharmacy Benefits, Drug Tiers & Formulary Rules

## Drug tiers

The HealthGuard formulary assigns every covered drug to one of five tiers. Your plan's SBC lists the exact cost for each tier.

| Tier | Description | Typical cost sharing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preferred generics | Lowest copay |
| 2 | Non-preferred generics, biosimilars and low-cost brands | Low copay |
| 3 | Preferred brands | Higher copay |
| 4 | Non-preferred brands and select specialty drugs | Coinsurance (% of cost) |
| 5 | Specialty drugs (biologics, oncology, MS) | Highest coinsurance |

Tier 1–3 drugs have flat copays on copay-based plans. Tier 4 and 5 drugs are charged coinsurance. On **Saver HDHP** plans you pay the full negotiated drug price until the deductible is met (except ACA preventive drugs, which are $0); on **Catastrophic** plans drugs are subject to the deductible.

Prescription costs count toward your out-of-pocket maximum (the medical and pharmacy maximum is integrated).

## 90-day supplies and mail order

Maintenance drugs can be filled for 90 days at retail or through **HealthGuard Home Delivery Pharmacy** for **2.5× the 30-day copay** (a savings of one copay per quarter). Specialty drugs must be filled through **HealthGuard Specialty Pharmacy** (1-800-447-4276), which offers free delivery and nurse support.

## Utilization management

- **Prior authorization (PA)** — the drug is covered only if clinical criteria are met. Look for `requires_prior_auth = true` in the formulary. Examples: Ozempic, Mounjaro and Trulicity (type 2 diabetes with A1c above goal on metformin), Wegovy and Zepbound (weight management, tier 4), Humira, Enbrel, Stelara, Dupixent, Repatha.
- **Step therapy** — you must try a preferred first-line drug before a later-step drug is covered. Rejected fills show reason **D011**.

- **Quantity limits** — maximum quantity per fill (for example, Ozempic 4 pens per 28 days; sumatriptan 9 tablets per 30 days).
- **Generic substitution** — when a generic is available, the pharmacy dispenses it unless your prescriber writes "dispense as written". If you request the brand anyway, you pay the brand copay plus the cost difference.

## GLP-1 medications

- **Type 2 diabetes** (Ozempic, Mounjaro, Trulicity — tier 3): prior authorization requires a type 2 diabetes diagnosis and an A1c above 7% after at least 90 days of metformin (or documented intolerance). Approvals last 12 months.
- **Weight management** (Wegovy, Zepbound — tier 4): prior authorization requires BMI ≥ 30 (or ≥ 27 with a weight-related condition), participation in a lifestyle program, and re-authorization after 6 months showing at least 5% weight loss.

## Insulin

Covered insulins cost **no more than $35 per 30-day supply** on all plans, including before the deductible on HDHP plans. Biosimilar insulin glargine (Semglee) is tier 2; Lantus and Humalog are tier 3.

## Formulary exceptions

If your drug is not on the formulary, or you need a quantity above the limit, your prescriber can request a **formulary exception** with supporting documentation. Standard requests are decided within 72 hours; expedited requests within 24 hours. Approved exceptions are covered at the tier 3 copay (or tier 5 coinsurance for specialty drugs).

## Formulary changes

The formulary is reviewed quarterly by the Pharmacy & Therapeutics Committee. If a drug you take is removed or moved to a higher tier mid-year, you receive 60 days' notice and a one-time 30-day transition fill.
