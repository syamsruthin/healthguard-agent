# Claims Filing, Processing & Explanation of Benefits

## 1. Who files the claim

**In-network care.** Your provider files the claim electronically (ANSI X12 837). You pay any copay at the visit; deductible and coinsurance amounts are billed to you after the claim is processed. You never need to file a claim for in-network care.

**Out-of-network care (Choice PPO and Saver HDHP only).** If the provider does not file for you, submit a claim yourself:
1. Get an **itemized bill** showing date of service, CPT/HCPCS procedure codes, ICD-10 diagnosis codes, the provider's name and NPI, and charges.
2. Complete **Form HG-1500** (member portal → Claims → Submit a claim).
3. Submit online at portal.healthguard.com, by mail to *HealthGuard Claims, P.O. Box 12345, Hartford, CT 06101*, or by fax to 1-860-555-0199.

Core HMO and Select EPO plans do not reimburse non-emergency out-of-network care.

## 2. Timely filing

Claims must be received within **180 calendar days of the date of service**. Late claims are denied with code **D003** unless you show good cause (for example, you were incapacitated or another insurer's coordination-of-benefits decision was pending).

## 3. How a claim is processed

1. **Eligibility check** — coverage active on the date of service (otherwise D005).
2. **Benefit determination** — procedure codes are mapped to your plan's benefit categories, visit limits and cost sharing.
3. **Authorization and referral checks** — services on the prior-authorization list need an approved authorization on file (otherwise D002); Core HMO specialist visits need a PCP referral (otherwise D010).
4. **Medical-necessity review** — select services are reviewed against InterQual/MCG criteria (D012 if not met).
5. **Coordination of benefits** — if you have other coverage, the primary payer is determined (D008 if information is missing).
6. **Duplicate detection** — repeat submissions are denied with D004 and need no action.

### Processing timelines

| Claim type | Decision timeline |
|---|---|
| Clean claim (complete and accurate) | 30 calendar days |
| Claim needing more information | 45 days after we receive the information |
| Urgent / concurrent care | 72 hours |
| Pre-service authorization request (standard) | 7 calendar days |
| Pre-service authorization request (expedited) | 72 hours |

A claim's status in the portal can be **pending** (received, not yet decided), **in_review** (clinical or COB review), **paid**, **denied**, or **appealed**.

## 4. Reading your Explanation of Benefits (EOB)

Each processed claim produces an EOB showing:
- **Billed amount** — what the provider charged.
- **Allowed amount** — the contracted rate HealthGuard recognizes. In-network providers accept it as payment in full.
- **Deductible applied**, **copay** and **coinsurance** — your cost-sharing components.
- **Member responsibility** — the total you owe the provider.
- **Plan paid** — HealthGuard's payment.

Amounts you pay for covered services count toward your deductible (deductible and coinsurance portions) and your out-of-pocket maximum (deductible, copays and coinsurance, including prescriptions). Premiums, non-covered services and balance bills from out-of-network providers do not count.

**Provider liability rule:** when an **in-network** provider fails to obtain a required prior authorization (D002) or referral (D010), or a claim is denied as not medically necessary (D012), the provider may **not** bill you for the denied amount. Out-of-network providers may bill you for denied services (except where the No Surprises Act applies).

## 6. If you disagree with a decision

You can file an appeal within **180 days** of the denial. See *Appeals & Grievances* for the full process, including external review by an Independent Review Organization.
