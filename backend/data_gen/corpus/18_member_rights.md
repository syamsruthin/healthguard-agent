# Member Rights & Responsibilities

## Your rights

### Right to information
You have the right to clear, accurate and timely information about your plan — the Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC), Evidence of Coverage (EOC), provider directory, formulary and any changes — in a culturally and linguistically appropriate manner (ACA Section 1557). Free interpreter services and documents in alternative formats are available.

### Right to choose providers
Choice PPO and Saver HDHP members may see any licensed provider (out-of-network at higher cost). Core HMO members choose a PCP and may change PCPs at any time (effective the first of the next month). Select EPO members may see any in-network provider without a referral. All members may see an in-network OB/GYN or behavioral health provider without a referral.

### Right to emergency care
You may receive emergency care at any hospital without prior authorization, at in-network cost sharing.

### Right to appeal and file grievances
You may appeal any denial (internal appeal within 180 days, then external review by an Independent Review Organization within 4 months) and file grievances about quality or service. See *Appeals, External Review & Grievances*.

### Right to privacy
Your protected health information (PHI) is safeguarded under HIPAA. See the *Notice of Privacy Practices*.

### Right to continuity of care
If your provider leaves the network during an active course of treatment, you may continue care at in-network cost sharing for up to 90 days (or through postpartum care).

### Right to participate in decisions
You have the right to discuss all treatment options with your providers regardless of cost or coverage, to refuse treatment, and to prepare advance directives.

### Right to a timely appointment
HealthGuard's access standards: urgent care within 48 hours, routine primary care within 10 business days, routine specialist and behavioral health care within 15 business days.

## Your responsibilities
- Show your member ID card when you receive care and keep your information current (report address, dependent or other-coverage changes within 30 days).
- Understand your benefits, cost sharing and exclusions.
- Make sure required **prior authorizations** and (on Core HMO) **referrals** are in place before non-emergency services.
- Pay premiums, deductibles, copays and coinsurance on time.
- Use in-network providers when possible to keep costs down.
- Follow the treatment plans you agree to with your providers.

## Non-discrimination
HealthGuard does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or religion.
