# Enrollment, Special Enrollment Periods, Premium Grace Periods & COBRA

## Open enrollment
Open enrollment for 2027 coverage runs **November 1, 2026 – January 15, 2027**. Enroll or change plans by December 15 for coverage starting January 1; enrollments between December 16 and January 15 start February 1.

## Special enrollment periods (SEP)
A qualifying life event lets you enroll or change plans outside open enrollment. You generally have **60 days** from the event to make the change:
- Losing other health coverage (job loss, aging off a parent's plan at 26, losing Medicaid)
- Marriage, birth, adoption or placement for adoption or foster care
- A permanent move to a new service area
- Gaining citizenship or lawful presence

**Birth or adoption:** coverage for the child is retroactive to the date of birth or adoption when you add them within 60 days. You may also switch the whole family to a different plan.

## Adding or removing dependents
Children can stay on a parent's plan until age **26**, regardless of student, marital or financial status. Report changes in address, dependent status or other coverage within **30 days**.

## Premium payment and grace periods
Premiums are due on the 1st of each month. If you miss a payment, you have a **31-day grace period** (or **3 months** if you receive advance premium tax credits). Claims during the second and third months of the extended grace period may be pended until premiums are paid. Coverage terminates if premiums are not paid by the end of the grace period.

## COBRA continuation coverage (employer group plans)
If you had HealthGuard coverage through an employer with 20 or more employees and lose it because of job loss (other than gross misconduct) or reduced hours, you may continue the same coverage under **COBRA**:
- You have **60 days** from the later of the loss of coverage or the COBRA notice to **elect** COBRA.
- Coverage lasts up to **18 months** (job loss or reduced hours), up to **29 months** with a disability extension, or up to **36 months** for dependents after divorce, the employee's death or Medicare entitlement.
- You pay up to **102%** of the full premium. The first payment is due within 45 days of election.
- COBRA coverage is retroactive to the date coverage ended, so no gap occurs if you elect within the window.

Losing COBRA because the maximum period ends is a qualifying event for a special enrollment period; voluntarily dropping COBRA is not.

## Termination
Coverage ends at the end of the month in which you request termination, lose eligibility, or the grace period expires. Members receive a certificate of coverage upon request.
