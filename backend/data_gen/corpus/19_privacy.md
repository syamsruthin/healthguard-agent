# Notice of Privacy Practices (HIPAA)

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information.

## How HealthGuard uses your information
- **Treatment** — sharing information with your providers to coordinate care.
- **Payment** — processing claims, determining eligibility, coordinating benefits.
- **Health care operations** — quality improvement, care management, fraud prevention, and customer service — including AI-assisted member support tools, which access only the minimum necessary information for the logged-in member.
- **As required by law** — public health reporting, court orders, law enforcement in limited circumstances.

HealthGuard does **not** sell your health information and does not use it for marketing without your written authorization.

## Your rights
- **Access** — get a copy of your claims and enrollment records (electronic copies within 30 days).
- **Amendment** — ask us to correct information you believe is wrong.
- **Accounting of disclosures** — a list of disclosures made in the last six years (excluding treatment, payment and operations).
- **Restrictions** — ask us to limit how we use or share your information.
- **Confidential communications** — ask to be contacted at a different address or phone.
- **Complaints** — file with HealthGuard's Privacy Office (privacy@healthguard.example, 1-800-446-3623) or the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. You will not be retaliated against for filing a complaint.

## Information about other members
For privacy, member services representatives and digital assistants can only discuss the account of the member who is verified (and dependents under 18 on the subscriber's plan). Spouses and adult dependents must authorize access with Form HG-AR1.

## Breach notification
You will be notified within 60 days if your unsecured health information is breached.
