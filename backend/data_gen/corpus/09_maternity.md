# Maternity & Newborn Care

## Prenatal and postnatal care — no cost
Routine prenatal visits, postpartum visits, standard prenatal lab work (including gestational diabetes screening), routine obstetric ultrasounds and fetal non-stress tests are covered **at no cost in-network on every plan**, including before the deductible on Saver HDHP plans.

Diagnostic tests that are not part of routine prenatal care (for example a comprehensive metabolic panel ordered for symptoms) follow your plan's diagnostic-test cost sharing.

## Genetic screening
Non-invasive prenatal testing (NIPT / cell-free DNA screening) and other prenatal genetic tests **require prior authorization** and are covered under the genetic testing benefit (deductible then coinsurance).

## Delivery
- Hospital delivery is covered under the **Childbirth/delivery facility services** benefit (inpatient cost sharing).
- **Pre-certification:** your OB should notify HealthGuard of the planned delivery hospital; the hospital must also notify HealthGuard within **24 hours** of admission.
- Federal law guarantees coverage for a hospital stay of at least **48 hours after a vaginal delivery** and **96 hours after a cesarean delivery**, without prior authorization for that length of stay.
- Birthing centers and certified nurse-midwives (CNM) in the network are covered.

## Newborn coverage
Your newborn is covered automatically for the first **31 days** after birth under your plan. To continue coverage you must **add the baby to your plan within 60 days of birth** (a qualifying life event — see *Enrollment, Special Enrollment & COBRA*). Coverage is retroactive to the date of birth. Newborn care in the hospital is subject to the newborn's own deductible on individual-to-family plan changes.

## Breastfeeding and postpartum support
Lactation consultant visits and one breast pump per pregnancy are covered at no cost. Postpartum depression screening is covered as preventive care, and treatment is covered under the mental health benefit.

## High-risk pregnancy program
Members with high-risk pregnancies can enroll in HealthGuard's free **Healthy Beginnings** nurse case management program (1-800-HG-NURSE).

## HSA tip
If you are on a Saver HDHP, delivery costs typically satisfy much of the deductible; you can pay your share with HSA funds tax-free.
