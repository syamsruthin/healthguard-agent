# Coordination of Benefits, Medicare & Subrogation

## When you have more than one plan
If you are covered by more than one health plan, coordination of benefits (COB) rules determine which plan pays first (**primary**) and which pays second (**secondary**). The secondary plan may pay some or all of the remaining balance, but total payments never exceed the allowed amount.

HealthGuard follows the National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) COB rules:
1. The plan covering you as an **employee or subscriber** is primary over a plan covering you as a dependent.
2. **Birthday rule for children:** when a child is covered under both parents' plans, the plan of the parent whose **birthday (month and day) falls earlier in the calendar year** is primary. The year of birth does not matter.
3. For children of **divorced or separated parents**, a court decree assigning responsibility controls; otherwise the custodial parent's plan is primary, then the custodial parent's spouse's plan, then the non-custodial parent's plan.
4. Active employee coverage is primary over COBRA or retiree coverage.
5. If none of the rules apply, the plan that has covered the person longer is primary.

Claims may be denied with **D008** until you complete the annual COB questionnaire in the member portal.

## Medicare
- If you are 65+ and covered through an active employer with **20 or more employees**, the employer plan is primary and Medicare secondary.
- With a smaller employer, or retiree coverage, Medicare is primary.
- Individual-market HealthGuard plans are not designed to coordinate with Medicare; contact Member Services when you become Medicare-eligible.

## Workers' compensation and auto accidents
Injuries covered by workers' compensation or auto insurance (including personal injury protection) are the responsibility of that insurer first. HealthGuard may pay claims while liability is determined.

## Subrogation and third-party recovery
If another party is responsible for your injury, HealthGuard has the right to recover payments it made from that party or their insurer (**subrogation**) or from any settlement you receive (**reimbursement**), as permitted by law. Please notify HealthGuard of any claim or lawsuit related to your injury.
