# Appeals, External Review & Grievances

You have the right to challenge any adverse benefit determination — a denied claim, a denied prior authorization, a reduced payment or a rescission of coverage.

## Level 1 — Internal appeal

- **Deadline to file:** within **180 calendar days** of receiving the denial (the date on your EOB or denial letter).
- **How to file:** member portal (Claims → Appeal this claim), by mail to *HealthGuard Appeals Department, P.O. Box 67890, Hartford, CT 06102*, or by phone at 1-800-HG-APPEAL (1-800-442-7732).
- **What to include:** your member ID and the claim or authorization number; a written statement explaining why you disagree; supporting documents such as a **letter of medical necessity** from your treating provider, medical records and test results.
- **Who decides:** a physician reviewer who was not involved in the original decision and is not a subordinate of the original reviewer.
- **Decision timelines:**
  - Pre-service (not yet received care): **30 calendar days**
  - Post-service (already received care): **60 calendar days**
  - Urgent care: **72 hours**
- You may review your claim file free of charge and submit new evidence at any time before the decision.

### Tips for common denial codes
- **D002 — prior authorization not obtained:** ask your provider to request a **retrospective authorization review** within 30 days of the denial; if it is approved the claim is reprocessed automatically. In-network providers cannot bill you for D002 denials.
- **D010 — referral required (Core HMO):** your PCP can submit a retroactive referral within 60 days of the date of service.
- **D012 — not medically necessary:** your provider may request a **peer-to-peer review** with the HealthGuard medical director before or during the appeal.
- **D009 — out-of-network on HMO/EPO:** explain whether the visit was an emergency or no in-network provider was reasonably available (a network-adequacy exception).

## Level 2 — External review

If the internal appeal is denied, you may request an **external review** by an **Independent Review Organization (IRO)** within **4 months** of the final internal decision.
- External review is **free** to the member.
- The IRO reviewer is a board-certified physician in the relevant specialty with no prior involvement.
- **Decision timeline:** **45 calendar days** (standard) or **72 hours** (expedited).
- The IRO decision is **binding** on HealthGuard.

## Expedited appeals

If waiting the standard time could seriously jeopardize your life, health or ability to regain maximum function, your physician can request an **expedited internal appeal and simultaneous expedited external review**, decided within **72 hours**. For ongoing treatment (concurrent care), coverage continues while the appeal is pending.

## Grievances

A grievance is a complaint about quality of care, access, customer service or other administrative issues (not a coverage decision). File a grievance within 180 days through Member Services or the portal. HealthGuard acknowledges grievances within **5 business days** and resolves them within **30 calendar days**.

## Help with your appeal

- Member Services: 1-800-HG-MEMBER (1-800-446-3623), Mon–Fri 8 AM–8 PM ET
- Your state Department of Insurance consumer assistance program can help you file.
- You may appoint an authorized representative (Form HG-AR1), including your provider.
